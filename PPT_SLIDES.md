# SLIDE 3 — INTRODUCTION

## What is Paper and Pulp?

- **Paper** — manufactured from wood fibers reduced into pulp, formed into a mat, compressed, and dried
- **Pulp** — fibrous material from wood or non-wood sources (cotton, bagasse, rice straw) via chemical or mechanical processes

### Paper Grades:
| Grade | Examples |
|---|---|
| Printing & Writing | Bond paper, notebooks |
| Newsprint | Newspapers |
| Corrugating/Containerboard | Linerboard, fluting |
| Tissue & Sanitary | Tissue, napkins |
| Specialty | Art paper, construction board |

---

### Philippine Context
- **Only integrated pulp mill** (PICOP Resources Inc.) **ceased operations in 2010**
- Industry now relies **primarily on recycled wastepaper**
- Wastepaper = **19% of total municipal solid waste** in the Philippines
- Mills supplement via **importation** of virgin pulp and wastepaper

# SLIDE 8 — DESIGN CONSTRAINTS

## ⚙️ 5 Key Constraints in Plant Design

### 1. Environmental & Regulatory
- Strict limits on **BOD, COD, TSS** of discharged water
- Air: control of **TRS (sulfur compounds)** and particulate matter
- Solid waste: ash, sludge, bark must be managed/utilized

### 2. Raw Material & Fiber
- Wood species availability dictates **cooking parameters**
- Alternative fibers require **de-inking and contaminant-removal systems**
- Large **chip storage yards** needed for continuous supply

### 3. Energy & Utility
- Mills must balance **steam from recovery boiler** vs. **electrical demands**
- Target: **Co-generation (CHP)** — energy self-sufficient or net exporter

### 4. Technical & Process
- Choose between **Chemical (Kraft/Sulfite)** vs. **Mechanical** pulping
- Must use **ECF or TCF** bleaching (no elemental chlorine)
- Corrosion-resistant materials: **duplex stainless steel / titanium**

### 5. Site & Geographical
- Must be near **water source** (river/dam) for intake and effluent
- Proximity to **transport infrastructure** (ports, highways, rail)

# SLIDE 10 — PULPING METHODS

## Method 1: Chemical Pulping (Kraft Process)

1. **Cooking** — Wood chips + White Liquor (NaOH + Na₂S) in digester at ~160°C, 10 atm → cellulose fibers separated from lignin
2. **Bleaching** — Residual lignin removed with chlorinate chemicals + caustic soda
3. **Chemical Recovery** — Black liquor → concentrated → burned in recovery boiler → energy + chemical recycling

---

## Method 2: Mechanical Pulping

1. **Delivery & Washing** — Chips conveyed, metal/sand removed
2. **Disaggregation (Refiner)** — Twin rotating disks grind chips into fibers
3. **Screening** — Foreign matter separated by size/gravity
4. **Bleaching** — Sodium hydrosulfite or hydrogen peroxide used
5. **Drainage** — Pulp thickened in chest

---

## Method 3: Wastepaper Pulping

1. **Defiberization** — Wastepaper + water in hydrapulper (3–5% concentration, 30–60 min)
2. **Removal of Foreign Objects** — Cleaners and screens
3. **Deinking** — Ink flaked off using alkali + surface-active agents
4. **Bleaching** — H₂O₂, sodium hydrosulfite, sodium hypochlorite (55–75% brightness)

---

# SLIDE 11 — STOCK PREPARATION

## 📦 Stock / Pulp Preparation

### Steps:
1. **Beating** — Disk refiner swells fibers; improves flexibility, surface area, sheet formation
2. **Blending** — Recycles broke; blends raw materials + recovered white water
3. **Addition of Chemicals & Fillers**
   - **Fillers** → opacity, brightness, smoothness
   - **Sizing agents** → ink resistance, water resistance
   - **Chemicals** → retention aids, strengthening agents, slime control, antifoaming, dyes

# SLIDE 12 — PAPERMAKING STAGE

## 📄 Papermaking — Step by Step

| Step | Equipment | Function |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Headbox** | Pressurized Headbox | Distributes fiber-water suspension uniformly across wire width |
| **2. Wire Section** | Fourdrinier Wire | Fibers settle; water drains → sheet formed |
| **3. Press Section** | Press Rolls + Felt | Mechanical dewatering; increases strength, improves surface |
| **4. Dryer Section** | Steam-heated Dryer Cylinders | Dries paper to 6–10% moisture content |
| **5. Size Press** | Size Press Machine | Applies starch for water resistance and surface strength |
| **6. Calendering** | Calender Stack | Smooths surface, adds gloss, uniform thickness |
| **7. Reel / Rewinder** | Pope Reel + Rewinder | Winds paper into jumbo rolls; trims to sellable widths |
